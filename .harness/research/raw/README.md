# Raw research archive

Store external research outputs here unchanged, with date, question, source/model/tool, and retrieval metadata. Raw research is evidence, not project authority.

Do not edit a raw item to make it agree with a later conclusion. Create a separate synthesis that identifies supporting, conflicting, missing, and time-sensitive evidence. Only an explicit owner decision or an approved governance artifact promotes a conclusion into project authority.

## Required metadata

- Research ID and date
- Exact question and decision it could change
- Search scope and sources
- Raw output or links, retained as received
- Known coverage limits
